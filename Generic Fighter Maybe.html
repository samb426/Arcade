Controls
Move — WASD (P1) · Arrows (P2)
Light / Medium / Heavy — J K L (P1) · Numpad 4 5 6 (P2)
Dash — I (P1) · Numpad 8 (P2)
Block — hold away  ·  Parry — tap forward just before a hit
Throw — Light + Medium  ·  Taunt — all three attacks
Gamepad works too, and everything is remappable in Options.
